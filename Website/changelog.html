3.1.12 Latest
Nov 9, 2026
  • Updates now come from a public Cloudflare feed, so they work for everyone.
  • Portable builds can update themselves too.
  • The auto-updater is pinned, so a release can’t quietly change how updates work.
3.1.8
Nov 9, 2026
  • Faster launch watchdog: the game starts on the first check, not after a delay.
  • VALORANT launch is confirmed instead of trusting Riot’s flag.
  • Add Account no longer reopens the Riot Client.
3.1.5
Nov 6, 2026
  • Smooth, inertial wheel scrolling.
  • Drag to reorder works on the whole card and no longer gets stuck.
  • Fixed a blank window when opening the portable build twice.
3.1.4
Nov 6, 2026
  • A “Quit VALORANT?” prompt before a switch. The game is never closed without asking.
  • Sign out of a single profile from its card.
  • Public release pipeline and WinGet submission.
3.1.1
Nov 6, 2026
  • Sessions are stored encrypted in ProgramData.
  • New app icons and a chevron taskbar icon.
  • Interface sounds toggle in Settings.
3.0.0
Nov 4, 2026
  • 30 animated Agent Mastery player cards, with a picker to change any card.
  • Add Account captures your live login in one step.
  • Rebuilt interface with profile cards, rank and win/loss.
