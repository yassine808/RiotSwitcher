Switch Riot accounts in one click.

No passwords, no logging out. RiotSwitcher keeps each login on your PC and opens the Riot Client already signed in.

Download for WindowsInstaller Portable .exe

It only touches the Riot Client. Never the game, never Vanguard.

This is the real app, running on dummy accounts. Press a card’s button.
