FAQ

Is RiotSwitcher free?

Yes. It is free and open source. If it saves you time you can leave a tip, but you never have to.

Where are my logins stored?

On your PC only, encrypted in ProgramData. Nothing is sent to a server.

Does it touch the game?

No. RiotSwitcher only works with the Riot Client. It never injects into or reads the game itself.

How do I add an account?

Sign in to the Riot Client once, then press Add Account. RiotSwitcher captures your live login in one step.

Which versions of Windows are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11.
